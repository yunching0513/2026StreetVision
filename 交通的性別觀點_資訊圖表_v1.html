01 — THE PATTERN · 趨勢觀察

女性是永續運輸的
沉默主力

Women are the silent backbone of sustainable transport.
交通並非性別中立的活動。台灣女性運具次數之公共運輸市占率歷年穩定為男性的 1.5 倍以上，2024 年差距更擴大至 1.75 倍。然而，這份對公共運輸的依賴並未換得相應的規劃支持。
Transport is not gender-neutral. Women in Taiwan have used public transit at least 1.5x the rate of men every year since 2009 — and 1.75x in 2024 — yet planning priorities still default to point-to-point male commuting patterns.

02 — THE MODE GAP · 運具差異

兩條截然不同的
移動軌跡

Two fundamentally different mobility paths.
臺北市調查顯示，女性通勤者選擇綠運輸的比例達 62.5%；男性則高度依賴機車（38.3%）與自用小客車（23.9%）。差異反映的不是偏好，而是成本、安全感與照顧責任的結構性差異。
In Taipei, 62.5% of female commuters use green modes, while men depend disproportionately on motorcycles (38.3%) and private cars (23.9%). What looks like preference is in fact a structural difference rooted in cost, safety, and care work.

03 — THE HIDDEN BURDEN · 隱形負擔

對公共運輸的依賴
不等於被公平對待

Dependence is not the same as being fairly served.
女性高比例使用公共運輸的同時，肩負職場與家庭照顧的雙重壓力。她們的出行不是線性的「家—公司」，而是接送、就醫、採買、照顧長輩等多節點任務的串聯。
High transit use comes alongside the dual burden of paid work and family care. Their journeys are not linear "home–office" lines but multi-node chains — school drop-off, errands, eldercare, return home.

行程串聯 — 被隱形的多節點旅次

Trip Chaining · Invisible Multi-Node Journeys
當男性的通勤是家 → 公司 → 家的兩段式線性旅次時，女性的通勤卻往往串聯起 4–7 個目的地。現行運具調查與大眾運輸路網設計，多以「點對點通勤」為基礎，無法服務這種「多目的、多節點、離峰時段」的旅次模式。

When a man's commute is a two-leg line, a woman's is a polygon — four to seven stops linking work, school drop-off, market, eldercare, and home. Most transit systems still serve the line.

A 1999 questionnaire that reshaped a city.

05 — WAY FORWARD · 政策路徑

從性別主流化到
照護型街道

From gender mainstreaming to care-centered streets.
交通規劃應納入性別視角。我們倡議五項可整合至《地方雙零自治條例》的具體政策，使台灣城市真正為所有人運作。
Five concrete proposals — designed to be embedded in the Local Dual-Zero Ordinance — to make Taiwanese cities work for everyone.
P · 01

性別影響評估入法

Mandatory Gender Impact Assessment
道路、人行道、大眾運輸場站之新建或整修工程應事前進行性別影響評估，將女性、照顧者、性別少數之需求納入規劃流程。
Require gender impact assessments for all road, pedestrian, and transit infrastructure projects — making the needs of women, caregivers, and gender minorities a structural input rather than an afterthought.
P · 02

統計增列非二元選項

Non-Binary Categories in Transport Statistics
交通部與各縣市運具調查應自下次調查（2026）起，增列「非二元」、「其他」、「拒答」選項，並公開多節點旅次數據，避免性別少數族群數據隱形化。
Add non-binary, other, and decline-to-answer options in the next National Travel Survey (2026). Publish multi-node trip data so gender-minority commuters are no longer statistically invisible.
P · 03

照護型街道試辦

Care-Centered Street Pilots
學區、醫療院所、社福機構周邊優先導入連續遮蔭、夜間照明、座椅密度、推車友善人行道，以「照顧者日常」為設計起點。
Roll out care-centered street pilots near schools, hospitals, and social-welfare facilities: continuous shading, night lighting, frequent seating, stroller-friendly sidewalks — designed around the daily lives of caregivers.
P · 04

多節點票證 · 離峰加班

Multi-Node Tickets & Off-Peak Frequency
研議「時段內可重複進出」票證設計，並強化離峰時段公車班次密度，回應 trip-chaining 多目的旅次模式。
Design tickets that allow re-entry within a time window, and increase off-peak bus frequency, to serve trip-chaining patterns that current commuter-centric networks ignore.
P · 05

夜間安全設計

Night-Time Safety By Design
場站照明、CCTV、緊急通報按鈕、女性夜間車廂等選項應作為標準配備而非可選項；建立性別交通使用者焦點團體，定期回饋路線與場站設計。
Lighting, CCTV, emergency buttons, and women's-carriage options as standard — not optional. Convene recurring focus groups of gender-diverse users to inform route and station design.
P · 06

通勤納入勞動條件

Commute As Labor Right
倡議將「通勤時間」納入勞動條件；輔導企業實施綠色通勤獎勵與彈性工時，回應 55% 女性「兼顧家庭身心俱疲」之結構壓力。
Treat commuting time as a labor-condition issue. Subsidize green-commute incentives and flexible work hours, addressing the 55% of women who report burnout from balancing day jobs with night caregiving.
